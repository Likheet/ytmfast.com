About

YTMFast is an independent software project with one aim: make YouTube Music on a PC cost almost nothing to run.

Why it exists

Music players used to be some of the lightest programs on a computer. Today most streaming apps are web pages inside a browser, and they use the CPU and memory to match. YTMFast rebuilds YouTube Music as a Windows app that idles at 0% CPU, stays under 200 MB of memory, and responds at once.
