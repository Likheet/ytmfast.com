About

YTFast is an independent software project with one aim: make YouTube Music on a computer open at once and cost almost nothing to run.

Why it exists

Music players used to be some of the lightest programs on a computer. Today most streaming apps are web pages inside a browser, and they use the CPU and memory to match. YTFast rebuilds YouTube Music as a native app that idles at 0% CPU, stays under 200 MB of memory, and starts songs in about a second.

Who is behind it

YTFast is built by Likheet Shetty.

It follows the path set by Spotifast, Carmine Paolino's app for Spotify, and is built on fastframe, the same foundation.

Where it stands

YTFast is at its third version, with builds for Windows and Mac. It is still being tested with real accounts.

The business

YTFast is the first product of a business that builds low-power desktop apps in Rust. The app is free to build yourself, with a paid ready-made version, and we take on custom apps for companies that want software this light. See pricing.
