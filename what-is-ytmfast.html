What is YTMFast?

YTMFast is YouTube Music rebuilt as a Windows app that uses as little of your computer as possible.

Download coming soon The Windows download is not posted yet. It will appear on the download page.

The numbers

MeasureYTMFast
CPU when idle0%
MemoryLess than 200 MB
Response to clicksInstant

These are the maker's own figures. Yours will vary with your PC and what is playing.

Who it is for

  • You keep music on all day. A player that is always open should cost almost nothing to run.
  • Your PC is already busy. Games, builds, and video calls need the CPU and memory more than a music player does.
  • You work on a laptop. Less work for the CPU means less heat and less drain on the battery.
  • You are tired of the browser tab. YTMFast has its own window and its own icon on the taskbar.

How it works

  1. DownloadGet the Windows app from the download page once it is posted.
  2. OpenYTMFast starts in its own window, separate from your browser.
  3. PlayListen to YouTube Music in a player that stays quick however long it has been open.
