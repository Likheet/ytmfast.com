Privacy

What this website collects, and what the YTFast app reads and sends. Last updated: 8 October 2026.

This website

  • No accounts. You cannot sign up or sign in on this site.
  • No analytics or advertising. The site loads no tracking scripts and sets no cookies.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and app downloads are served by GitHub. GitHub may record standard request details, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the service running and secure.
  • Fonts. The site's typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the same standard request details when your browser fetches it.

If you buy Pro

Payments are handled by PayPal. We never see your card details. PayPal shares your name, email address and the order with us, and we use them only to send your licence and to help with your order.

If you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not add you to a mailing list or share your address with anyone.

The YTFast app

What the app reads and sends, and where:

  • Your browser's sign-in. YTFast reads only YouTube's sign-in from the browser you choose. Every other site's sign-in data is dropped the moment it is read.
  • Where the sign-in goes. It stays on your computer and is only ever sent to YouTube.
  • Your plays. Songs you play are reported to YouTube, as they are on the website, so they appear in your History.
  • Lyrics. When the Lyrics tab is open and YouTube Music has no lyrics that follow the song, YTFast asks LRCLIB (lrclib.net) about the song playing.
  • Helper programs. On first run YTFast downloads yt-dlp and Deno and checks both against their published fingerprints.

Files the app keeps

Delete these folders to remove them.

  • Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\YtFast
  • Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/YtFast and ~/Library/Caches/YtFast
