Macs with an Intel processor are not supported yet. On Windows, Chrome, Edge and Brave lock their sign-in data so that other programs cannot read it. That is why YTFast needs Firefox there.

What you will need

  • A YouTube Music Premium account.
  • A browser signed in to music.youtube.com with that account.
  • About 250 MB of disk space. The first time you sign in, YTFast downloads two helper programs, yt-dlp and Deno.
  • An internet connection.

Open it on Windows

  1. Unzip itRight-click the zip, choose Extract All, then open the extracted folder.
  2. Open YTFast.exeIf Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. Windows asks this once for each new copy, because YTFast is not yet signed with a paid certificate.
  3. Choose FirefoxClick Continue. YTFast reads your YouTube Music sign-in from it.

To keep it handy, move YTFast.exe to a folder of your choice, then right-click it and choose Pin to Start or Pin to taskbar.

Open it on a Mac

  1. Unzip itIn Downloads, double-click what you downloaded. In the folder that opens, double-click YTFast-mac.zip.
  2. Move it to ApplicationsDrag YTFast into your Applications folder.
  3. Allow it onceOpen it. Your Mac says it could not check the app: click Done. Then open System Settings, click Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to the message about YTFast. You do this once for each new copy.
  4. Choose your browserWith Chrome, Edge or Brave, your Mac asks to allow access to the browser's Safe Storage: type your Mac password and click Always Allow. With Safari, click Open Full Disk Access when asked and turn on YTFast.

The first time you sign in

YTFast fetches its two helper programs and gets YouTube Music's player ready. This takes a minute or two and happens once. After that, songs start in about a second, and YTFast signs in by itself each time it opens.
