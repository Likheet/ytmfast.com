What is YTFast?

YTFast (YouTube Music Fast) is a desktop app for YouTube Music. It does what the YouTube Music app does, except video, with its own screens instead of a web page.

Early version YTFast is at its third version and is still being tested with real accounts. Expect rough edges, and read what to know before you use it.

The numbers

MeasureYTFast
Time to openUnder a second is the aim
Time for a song to startAbout a second
CPU when idle or paused0%
MemoryLess than 200 MB

These are the maker's own figures. Yours will vary with your computer and your connection.

What you get

  • The layout you know. A bar across the top with search and your account, a menu on the left with Home, Explore, Library and your playlists, and the player bar along the bottom.
  • Pages for everything. Albums, artists, playlists and moods, with search that suggests as you type.
  • A player page. The cover on the left over its own colours, with Up next, Lyrics and Related on the right.
  • Lyrics that follow the song. Click a line to jump to it.
  • Your library. Like and dislike songs, save albums and playlists, subscribe to artists, and make, rename, edit and delete playlists.
  • A real queue. Play next, add to queue, reorder Up next, start a radio, shuffle and repeat.
  • Autoplay. When the queue runs out, songs like the last one follow. You can turn this off.

How it works

  • Your sign-in comes from your web browser. Only YouTube's part is read. It stays on your computer and is only sent to YouTube.
  • Your music comes from the same internal service that music.youtube.com uses. YouTube Music has no official API.
  • The audio of each song is found the way the website finds it, with help from yt-dlp, and plays while it downloads.
  • Lyrics come from YouTube Music, or from LRCLIB, a free lyrics database, when YouTube Music has none that follow the song.
  • Playback happens in YTFast itself, through the same audio code as Spotifast. That is why it uses no CPU while paused.
  • Your plays are reported to YouTube, so History, Home and your mixes keep learning.

Left out on purpose

Videos, offline downloads, casting and the Samples feed.

Before you use it

  • You need YouTube Music Premium. YTFast does not work with free accounts.
  • It is unofficial. YTFast is not made by Google or YouTube, and using unofficial apps is against YouTube's terms of service.
  • Your account carries some risk. The makers of yt-dlp warn that an account used with it can be banned, temporarily or permanently. YTFast keeps to a normal listener's pace and never downloads in bulk, but the risk is not zero.
  • YouTube changes. When YouTube changes how the website works, playback can break until YTFast or yt-dlp catches up.

Credits

YTFast builds on fastframe and learns from Spotifast, ytmusicapi, Kaset and yt-dlp. Its look is inspired by a theme from Better Lyrics, recreated without its code. Icons are from Lucide.
