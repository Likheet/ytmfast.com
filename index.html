Why rebuild it?

YouTube Music on a computer usually means a browser tab, and a browser tab carries the weight of a whole web page even when all it does is play a song. YTMFast is YouTube Music rebuilt as a Windows program that costs your computer almost nothing: 0% CPU when idle, less than 200 MB of memory, and an interface that reacts at once.
