Performance

How light is it?

YTMFast idles at 0% CPU and uses less than 200 MB of memory. These are the maker's own figures, and yours will vary with your PC and what is playing.

How is it different from YouTube Music in a browser?

A browser tab carries a whole web page even when it is only playing a song. YTMFast is a separate Windows program with its own window and taskbar icon, rebuilt to use far less CPU and memory and to respond at once.

Why does a music player need to be light?

Because it is open all day. Whatever a player uses, it takes from the games, calls, and work running beside it, and on a laptop it takes from the battery too.
